How to run it alongside class

Rules that keep this sustainable

Class first, this second

Do the supplemental after class, or at another point in the day. The Tuesday aerobic work suits a separate slot best, since easy volume is what it needs.

Class overlaps? Swap, don't stack

Mayhem programmes a heavy squat or clean most weeks — a 3-rep back squat, an 8-rep front squat, cleans off the rack. When it lands on a day you also squat here, do the class lift and skip mine. Never do both.

Squat days are the first thing to cut

Two squat sessions plus class squats is the fastest way into a hole at 40+. If your legs are flat on Thursday, drop the back squat to 3 sets at RPE 6 or skip it. The Monday clean and front squat is the one that has to happen.

Engine work is paced, not tested

Z2 means you can say full sentences. Most people run it too hard. Save hard efforts for the intervals and sims.

Deloads are dashed

Weeks 4 and 8 cut volume by ~40%. Take them even if you feel good; that's when the adaptation happens at 40+.

Auto-regulate Friday

If you're sore or sleep was poor, turn Friday into 40 min Z2 bike/ski. Missing one threshold day costs nothing, but digging a hole does.

Fuel the extra 5 hours

That's roughly 5 extra training hours a week. Carbs around sessions and enough sleep decide whether it turns into fitness.
